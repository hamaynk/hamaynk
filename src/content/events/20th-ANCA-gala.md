Join us for the 20th Annual Armenian National Committee of America Gala in Warwick, Rhode Island. This prestigious event raises vital funds for the ANCA Eastern Region Endowment Fund, supporting critical advocacy, education, and civic engagement initiatives that advance the Armenian Cause throughout the Eastern United States.

Presented by the Armenian National Committee of Rhode Island, this year’s gala continues the tradition of bringing together community leaders, activists, supporters, and public officials to celebrate achievements and strengthen regional connections.

Attendees are invited to enjoy an elegant evening featuring outstanding dining, live entertainment, and meaningful recognition of distinguished service through the ANCA Eastern Region’s prestigious award ceremonies.
