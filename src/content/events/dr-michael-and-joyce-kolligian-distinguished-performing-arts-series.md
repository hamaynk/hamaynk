Kariné Poghosyan is an acclaimed Armenian-American concert pianist praised for her passionate and captivating performances. She has appeared at renowned venues including Carnegie Hall, Vienna’s Musikverein, and the Chicago Cultural Center. Her award-winning recordings have carned critical acclaim from *Gramophone Magazine* and the *American Record Guide*.
