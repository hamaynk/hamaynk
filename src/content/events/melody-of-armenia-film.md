On Saturday, September 12th, the Armenian Cultural Association of Rhode Island (ACAA-RI) and the Providence Hamazkayin will be hosting a screening of *The Melody of Armenia*.

U. George Aghjayan, Director of the ARF Archives, will lead the restoration efforts of the film and will be present to discuss how the film came to be, along with a Q&A session following the screening.

--------------------------

**About the Film:**

*The Melody of Armenia* is a recently restored 100-year-old film, a remarkable piece of Armenian history brought back to life. Originally filmed in the 1930s and thought lost, this six-reel silent film was rediscovered in the ARF Archives in Watertown and painstakingly restored.

The film contains incredibly rare footage including:
- The Burning of Smyrna in 1922
- Armenian community life in France in the 1920s
- Images of Armenian schools
- Homenetmen scouts in the 1930s
- Prominent figures of the First Republic (Avedis Aharonian, Alexander Khatisian, General Andranik, and more)

The restored film is accompanied by a newly composed Armenian soundtrack featuring duduk, clarinet, violin, cello, and piano. The score was composed by Mher Mnatsakanyan, a world-class educator and performer.
