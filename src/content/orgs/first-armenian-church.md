The First Armenian Church is a foundational parish of the Armenian Evangelical Christian tradition located in Belmont, Massachusetts. Originally founded in 1891 by a group of Armenian immigrants gathering in Boston, its central purpose is to proclaim the Gospel of salvation, nurture spiritual growth, and share the love of Jesus Christ while celebrating its rich cultural heritage. Operating out of its current Belmont facility since 1958, the church anchors its community through dynamic Sunday worship services in English, a vibrant Sunday School, youth Christian Endeavor programs, and active missions and community outreach initiatives.
