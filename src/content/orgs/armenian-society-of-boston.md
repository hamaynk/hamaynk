The Armenian Society of Boston is a dedicated cultural and civic non-profit organization serving the diaspora across the greater Boston area. Its central purpose is to create a dynamic networking and social community focused on preserving Armenian traditions, language, and cultural values for future generations. Acting as a bridge between historical identity and modern civic engagement, the organization anchors its community by hosting cultural gatherings, sponsoring educational forums, and curating local artistic events—such as community film screenings—that honor heritage while fostering unity among Armenian-Americans.
