The Armenian American Pharmacists’ Association (AAPA) is a social/charitable non-profit organization of pharmacists of Armenian heritage based in Boston, Massachusetts. It was founded on June 2, 1981 by Harold Partamian, the organization’s first President.

Members of the AAPA are united by their commitment to:
- Gathering with fellow pharmacist colleagues to participate in continuing education events, to network, and to cultivate lifelong friendships
- Contributing to the Armenian community through health care education and advocacy.
