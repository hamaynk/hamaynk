The Armenian Cultural Association of Maine is a non-profit organization founded in 2003 to support one of the oldest Armenian diaspora communities in the United States. Based in the Portland area, its central purpose is to cultivate, celebrate, and preserve Armenian history and cultural heritage. Through community lectures, annual holiday traditions, summer picnics, and the maintenance of a dedicated genocide memorial monument, the association fosters local fellowship and raises public awareness about Armenian identity.
