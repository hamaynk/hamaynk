The Armenian Cultural Foundation is a private museum and research library located in a historic Greek Revival mansion in Arlington, Massachusetts. Founded in 1945, its purpose is to preserve and enhance Armenian history, literature, and letters, with a unique emphasis on the history of Armenian music in the diaspora and the role of women in Armenian history. Holding over 45,000 volumes, it serves as an international resource for academics and bibliophiles through its extensive collection of rare classical texts, old-world periodicals, and scholarly arts programming.
