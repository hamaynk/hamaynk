The Armenian Church of the Holy Translators serves to nurture the spiritual, cultural, and educational life of Armenian Christians in the MetroWest Massachusetts area. As a parish of the Armenian Apostolic Church, it preserves ancient orthodox traditions and liturgy while fostering community connection. Inspired by its namesake 5th-century saints, the church emphasizes learning by offering youth education and heritage programs to pass Armenian identity down to future generations.
