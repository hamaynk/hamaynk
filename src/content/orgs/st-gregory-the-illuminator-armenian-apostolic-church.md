The St. Gregory Armenian Apostolic Church—formally dedicated to Saint Gregory the Illuminator—is a historic parish located in the Indian Orchard neighborhood of Springfield, Massachusetts. Celebrating its 90th anniversary, its core purpose is to preach the Gospel of Jesus Christ and serve as a spiritual home for the Armenian faithful in Western Massachusetts. Under the jurisdiction of the Eastern Prelacy of the Armenian Apostolic Church, the parish preserves traditional Orthodox Divine Liturgy, hosts community fellowships, and provides educational resources to keep Armenian spiritual identity and cultural heritage alive across generations.
