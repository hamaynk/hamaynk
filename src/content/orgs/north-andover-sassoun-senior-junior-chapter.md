Founded in 1997, the North Andover “Sassoun” chapter works to unite Armenian youth and organize activities in the North Andover, MA area. The North Andover “Sassoun” sets out to achieve their goals and objectives throughout the year, including events such as our annual Easter Bake Sale fundraiser, annual College Basketball March Madness Fundraiser, chapter outings/social events, and participation in Eastern Region events such as Senior Olympics, JAG, JWO, Junior & Senior Seminar, NATs and Camp Haiastan!
