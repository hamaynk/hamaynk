The Holy Trinity Armenian Church is an active parish of the Eastern Diocese of the Armenian Church of America located in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Consecrated in 1961 along Brattle Street, its purpose is to propagate the life and teachings of Jesus Christ while serving as a prominent home for traditional Armenian Apostolic worship. Designed in a stunning Eastern Christian style, the church anchors the local diaspora through its dedicated Sunday and Armenian language schools, vibrant youth group (ACYOA) initiatives, and annual heritage events like its massive community music festivals and picnics.
