The Boston Chapter of Hamazkayin (the Armenian Educational and Cultural Society) is a prominent non-profit organization serving the Armenian diaspora in Greater Boston, primarily out of Watertown, Massachusetts. Operating under a global network founded in 1928, its central purpose is to preserve and promote Armenian language, music, film, and theater. Through dynamic initiatives—including the Gorani ethnographic dance classes, the ArtNova adult choir, and the Children’s Voice Academy—the chapter actively protects Armenian identity and engages the local community across generations.
