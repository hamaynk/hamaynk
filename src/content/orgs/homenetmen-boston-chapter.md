The Homenetmen Boston Chapter is a non-profit organization established in 1923 to serve Armenian-American youth in Greater Boston. Operating under the global Homenetmen network with the motto "Rise and Raise," its purpose is to provide physical and social education outside the classroom. Through scouting troops, sports programs, and cultural traditions, the chapter builds leadership skills and instills a deep, lifelong connection to Armenian heritage.
