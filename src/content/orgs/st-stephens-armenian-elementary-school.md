The St. Stephen's Armenian Elementary School is an independent, coeducational private school serving the Armenian-American community from its campus in Watertown, Massachusetts. Founded in 1984, it is the only bilingual Armenian day school in New England and is fully accredited by the Association of Independent Schools in New England (AISNE). Its core purpose is to provide an excellent preschool-through-fifth-grade American academic foundation while instilling a deep appreciation for Armenian culture, history, and language.
