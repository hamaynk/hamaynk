The Pan Armenian Council of New England is a prominent activist and community coalition serving the greater Boston area and broader region. Originally formed in October 2022 by local leadership, its central purpose is to unify Boston’s vast Armenian resources, raise civic awareness, and advocate for global Armenian human rights and sovereignty. The coalition anchors its community by organizing large-scale advocacy efforts—such as demonstrations at the Massachusetts State House—and coordinating major humanitarian initiatives, including collaborative benefit concerts at Boston's Symphony Hall to aid displaced families.
