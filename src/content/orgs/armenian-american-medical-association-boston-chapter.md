The Armenian American Medical Association (Boston Chapter) is a prominent non-profit organization representing Armenian-American healthcare professionals throughout the greater Boston area and New England. Established in 1972, its central purpose is to promote excellence in healthcare across the communities it serves while advancing the professional development of its members. The association anchors its professional and lay networks by offering medical student scholarships, hosting collaborative networking events, providing community health guidance, and partnering with regional universities to foster medical education, research, and healthcare delivery initiatives directly in Armenia.
