The St. Stephen's Armenian Saturday School is a long-standing, one-day educational program operated under the jurisdiction of St. Stephen's Armenian Apostolic Church in Watertown, Massachusetts. In continuous existence for over 70 years, its core purpose is to provide children from early childhood through high school with an immersive environment to study the Armenian language, history, literature, and traditional dance. Operating out of the Armenian Cultural and Educational Center campus, the school caters to youth who may not attend a full-time day school. It allows them to practice their native tongue among peers, participate in church holiday services, and cultivate a durable connection to their ancestral heritage.
