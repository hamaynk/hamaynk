The MENK Calendar is a vital digital community service and information framework serving the Armenian diaspora across the broader New England region. Originally launched in 2001 as a dedicated initiative of Hamazkayin-Boston, its central purpose—reflected in its name, which means “we” or “togetherness” in Armenian—is to unify local splinter groups and build a cohesive sense of cultural belonging. Serving thousands of readers, the platform anchors its community by publishing a comprehensive, free weekly online event directory and email dispatch that coordinates and tracks the diverse activities, religious services, and cultural gatherings of New England’s multifaceted Armenian organizations.
