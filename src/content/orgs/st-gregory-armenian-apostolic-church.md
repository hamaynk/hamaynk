The St. Gregory Armenian Apostolic Church is a historic parish located in the Indian Orchard neighborhood of Springfield, Massachusetts. Having celebrated its 90th anniversary in 2024, the church serves as an enduring spiritual and cultural anchor for the Armenian diaspora in Western Massachusetts. Through traditional Orthodox divine liturgy, community fellowship, and heritage preservation, the parish is dedicated to cultivating the Armenian Christian faith and passing down its rich cultural identity to future generations.
