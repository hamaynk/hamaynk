The AYF Greater Boston “Nejdeh” Senior & Junior Chapter is a prominent branch of the Armenian Youth Federation serving young Armenian-Americans in the Greater Boston area. Formed in 2000 by merging the historical Watertown and Boston chapters, its purpose is to keep local youth deeply engaged with the Armenian cause through its five core pillars: athletics, culture, education, politics, and social networking. The chapter frequently hosts educational meetings, political advocacy initiatives, and highly competitive sports programs—famously dominating regional and national AYF Olympic events—to build leadership skills and lasting heritage bonds.
