The Armenian Memorial Church is a foundational parish of the Armenian Evangelical Christian tradition located in Watertown, Massachusetts. Originally tracing its heritage to immigrant Bible study groups in 1897, its central purpose is to worship God, proclaim the Gospel, and nurture a multigenerational faith community. Formally incorporating in 1915 to honor the victims of the Armenian Genocide, the church has operated out of its dedicated Watertown sanctuary since 1950. Today, it anchors its community through warm Sunday worship services, an active youth group, a vibrant Sunday School, and specialized music ministries.
