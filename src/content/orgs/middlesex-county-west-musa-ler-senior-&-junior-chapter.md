The Middlesex County West “Musa Ler” Senior & Junior Chapter is a local branch of the Armenian Youth Federation (AYF-YOARF) serving youth across the western suburbs of Boston, Massachusetts. Named in honor of the historic defense of Musa Dagh, the chapter's purpose is to advance the social, athletic, and political awareness of Armenian-American youth. By organizing annual community dances, leadership seminars, and participating in regional athletic events, it provides a crucial space for young people to strengthen their peer networks and actively engage with their cultural heritage.
