The AGBU Young Professionals (YP) Boston is a vibrant regional chapter of the AGBU Young Professionals Network, operating out of the Armenian General Benevolent Union Center in Watertown, Massachusetts. Originally established as part of a global movement to engage the next generation, its central purpose is to preserve Armenian heritage and identity while fostering professional growth for the diaspora’s young adults. Serving the greater Boston area, the organization anchors its community by providing targeted networking forums, cultural education, humanitarian fundraising initiatives, and hosting participants of the annual AGBU Global Leadership Program summer internships.
