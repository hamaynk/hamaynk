This organization’s mission is to build a sense of community among Armenian Americans living in and around metropolitan Boston. They provide a safe environment and let other organizations host concerts, exhibits, lectures, and gatherings. They are an everyday Armenian school, Saturday and Sunday educational programs for children and adults, as well as after school activities such as music instructions, dance classes and Scouting programs. Their goal is to help the development of good citizenship and the prevention of juvenile delinquency through the promotion of youth-oriented programs and activities which foster awareness in the Armenian heritage and American Civil Society.
