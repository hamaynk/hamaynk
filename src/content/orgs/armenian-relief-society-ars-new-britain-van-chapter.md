The Armenian Relief Society (ARS) New Britain "Van" Chapter is a humanitarian non-profit organization based in New Britain, Connecticut, operating under the ARS of Eastern USA. Officially chartered in May 2024 at St. Stephen's Armenian Apostolic Church, its central purpose is to provide social services, financial aid, and educational resources while preserving Armenian identity. Serving the local diaspora, the organization anchors its community by mobilizing volunteers to fundraise for local student scholarships and global humanitarian relief projects.
