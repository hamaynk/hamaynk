St. George Armenian Apostolic Church is a foundational parish of the Diocese of the Armenian Church of America located in Hartford, Connecticut. Originally tracing its roots to the late 19th century when the first Armenian immigrants settled in the capital city, its central purpose is to preserve the orthodox faith, share the Gospel of Christ, and cultivate a deep connection to Armenian Christian heritage. Consecrated in its permanent home in 1953, the parish has anchored its community for over seven decades through traditional Badarak services, a dedicated Sunday School, community banquets, and local youth sports clinics.
