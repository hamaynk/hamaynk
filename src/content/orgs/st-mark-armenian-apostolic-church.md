The St. Mark Armenian Apostolic Church is a vibrant parish of the Eastern Diocese of the Armenian Church of America located in Springfield, Massachusetts. Consecrated in 1983, the church features traditional Armenian architecture and serves as a vital spiritual home for the Armenian community in the western part of the state. Through regular divine liturgy, youth education, and cultural events like its annual food festival, the parish actively preserves the ancient Orthodox faith, heritage, and identity of the Armenian people for future generations.
