The *Armenian Weekly* is a premier English-language publication and a vital intellectual pillar of the global diaspora, based in Watertown, Massachusetts. Originally established in June 1934 as an English-language page within its sister Armenian-language newspaper, *Hairenik*, its central purpose is to provide authoritative, independent news coverage, political analysis, and a platform for literary expression for English-speaking generations of Armenians. Published by the Hairenik Association, the news outlet anchors its worldwide readership by delivering critical reporting on Armenian-American civic life, historical research, and breaking geopolitical developments across Armenia, Artsakh, and the international diaspora.
