NAASR supports Armenian Studies in all of its diversity, including history, art, literature, language, and politics, from ancient times to contemporary issues.

Founded in 1955, NAASR is an independent organization that funds research, cultivates emerging scholars, houses the 40,000-volume Mardigian Library, and offers a wide array of public events, all under one roof in Belmont.

NAASR organized the NextGen Program that gave us the opportunity to build Hamaynk!
