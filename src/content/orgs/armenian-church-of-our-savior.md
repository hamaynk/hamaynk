The Armenian Church of Our Saviour is a historic parish of the Eastern Diocese of the Armenian Church of America located in Worcester, Massachusetts. Consecrated in 1891, it holds the profound distinction of being the first Armenian Apostolic church established in the United States. Its central purpose is to provide a spiritual and cultural sanctuary for the Worcester Armenian community, preserving ancient Orthodox traditions through weekly Divine Liturgy, youth religious education, and large heritage milestones like its annual food festival.
